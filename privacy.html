Privacy Policy

Shorts Studio · Last updated: 14 November 2026

Shorts Studio is a private tool operated by a single owner for their own YouTube channel and Google Drive. It is not offered to other users.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube: the owner's channel information and analytics (read-only), and the ability to upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • Google Drive: files in the owner's own Drive, used to store and archive the owner's video files and project assets.
  • Public YouTube data: public video and channel statistics, used for topic research.

How the data is used

Data is used only to plan, produce, upload and analyse the owner's own videos. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored as private environment secrets and are never published. Research reports are stored privately by the owner.

Google API Services

Shorts Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube features are governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
