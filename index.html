Shorts Studio

Shorts Studio is a private, single-owner tool used to research, produce and manage short educational videos for the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Reads public YouTube data and the owner's own channel analytics to plan video topics.
  • Uploads finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, always as private first.
  • Stores and archives the owner's own video files in the owner's own Google Drive.

The app is not offered to the public. It only accesses accounts belonging to its owner.
